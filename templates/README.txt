AASX files placed in this folder are uploaded automatically when the server starts (AAS_TEMPLATES_DIR).
Download the official IDTA submodel templates from https://industrialdigitaltwin.org/content-hub/
Most are not bundled in this repository. The product passport templates (IDTA 02099-1 and the battery passport
IDTA 02035-1 to 02035-7) are shipped unmodified in apps/web/public/samples/idta with their CC BY 4.0 attribution
(NOTICE.md) — copy them here as they are if the server should also put them in the library.

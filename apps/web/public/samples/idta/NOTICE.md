**Industrial Digital Twin Association (IDTA)**, Lyoner Strasse 18, 60528 Frankfurt am Main, Germany.

All eight files are serialized with the XML namespace of metamodel 3.1 (`https://admin-shell.io/aas/3/1`).
This tool reads them as they are and saves them as JSON. Using them helps write and check passport data;
it does not by itself make a product compliant with the EU Digital Product Passport rules.
